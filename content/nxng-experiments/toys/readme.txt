.SOME WORDS ABOUT THE DEMO

 You can watch a windows demo made in 6 weeks of VERY intensive work!
 After Incoming Future released at The Party'98, we have looked to improve
 cinematic engagement in the demo scene. From this way, we now only get
 attentious though 3d card accelerator and this demo is that result.

 You need a 3d card to watch this demo.
 It support for Direct3D/DirectSound.
 The minimal requirement is Pentium II 266mhz, Voodoo chipset

 MP3 audio compression is used.
 Decompression for playing is fully managed using G-Audio, a very convivial
 stream audio replayer for Windows. It support multi streaming with full
 DirectSound improvement (the only one) for optimal performance and fiability.
 You can get it for free on our site, Olympus2.
